Sit still. Your face does the rest.

pulse frame is a heart rate monitor that runs on your webcam. No watch, no strap, no finger clip. Just you, a camera and about three minutes.

How it works

  1. 1
    Sit in front of the camera.

    Face a window or a lamp so the light on your face is even.

  2. 2
    It watches your skin.

    Each heartbeat sends a little more blood to your face, and your skin's colour shifts ever so slightly. Too small for you to see, not too small for a camera.

  3. 3
    It counts the beats.

    Those tiny shifts become a pulse wave, and pulse frame counts the beats over a minute and a half of sitting still.

  4. 4
    You get your numbers.

    Heart rate and two variation numbers, no wearable needed.

What you get

BPMHeart rate

Beats per minute over the last counted minute, with a likely range around it.

msBeat to beat variation

How much the gap between your beats shifts from one beat to the next.

msOverall rhythm variation

How much the gaps between your beats spread out over a minute.

Both variation numbers are in ms (thousandths of a second). Compare them only with your own runs.

For wellness and curiosity only. pulse frame is not a medical device, and its numbers are estimates.
