# Security policy

pulse-frame is a localhost research experiment. It reads a heart rate off a webcam for fun and science. It is not a medical device, so do not trust it with anything that matters to your health.

## Supported versions

None yet. There are no releases, only `master`. Fixes land there and nowhere else.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Found a hole? Do not open a public issue for it.

Report it privately instead: open the repository's Security tab and click "Report a vulnerability". That is GitHub's private vulnerability reporting, and it keeps the details out of sight until a fix is out.

Say what you found, how to reproduce it, and what you think it lets someone do. You will get a reply in the advisory, and credit if you want it.
